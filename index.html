Part 1: Brand brief

1. What it is

Tekna is the personal brand of Tuba, a designer-engineer who turns early ideas into real startups and digital products. The name comes from techne, Greek for craft: technology made with a maker's care.

2. Who it is for

  • Small businesses in Japan and India taking their first serious step online
  • Student and early-stage startup teams (university projects, hackathons) who need someone to turn an idea into a working product
  • Cross-border teams working between Japan, India and the rest of the world
  • Peers and collaborators in the design and tech community

3. How it should feel

Calm · Precise · Vibrant · Joyful · Refined

4. How it must not feel

Corporate or cold · Generic tech-bro (dark mode + neon gradients) · Cheap or templated

5. Where people will see it

Portfolio website · CV · Pitch decks and slides · Instagram · LinkedIn · X

Concept

"Light through structure." Carved jaali screens (India) and Tadao Ando's concrete and light (Japan), worn like clean techwear. Structure = tech, lace = craft.

Colour palette

Rule: about 75% light colours (Petal Cream, Peach, Fuji Lilac, Rose) and 25% strong colours (Kesari, Akane, Koki-murasaki). No black and no grey: text is Koki-murasaki, the deepest purple in the painting. The soft petals keep it relaxing and refined; saffron and Akane make it vibrant and playful.
